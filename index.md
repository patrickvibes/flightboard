# Flight Board

**See what's flying over you, right now.** Flight Board is a small ESP32
board with a 0.96" OLED screen that shows live aircraft overhead — callsign,
distance, altitude, speed and when each one will pass closest. It also serves
a full dashboard to any browser on your home network: a live map, a cockpit
view, an "overhead now" alert and more.

No app to install, no subscription. Data comes from the
[OpenSky Network](https://opensky-network.org), a community-run network of
aircraft receivers.

## What you get
- **On the device:** one flight at a time on the OLED, stepping with the
  button or rotating automatically, with a beep and LED flash for new
  arrivals, close approaches and emergency codes.
- **In your browser:** a live flight board, map, per-flight details (airline,
  route, aircraft type), a full-screen cockpit instrument panel, traffic
  history, session records and a wall-display kiosk mode.

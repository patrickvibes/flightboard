18:T8a1,<h1 id="faq">FAQ</h1>
<h3 id="does-it-pick-up-aircraft-radio-signals-itself">Does it pick up aircraft radio signals itself?</h3>
<p>No. It reads live positions from the OpenSky Network over the internet.
OpenSky's data comes from volunteers' receivers.</p>
<h3 id="do-i-need-an-account-or-subscription">Do I need an account or subscription?</h3>
<p>No, it works anonymously. A free OpenSky account is recommended so it can
run all day — see <a href="/flightboard/opensky-account/">OpenSky account</a>.</p>
<h3 id="can-i-see-it-from-outside-my-home">Can I see it from outside my home?</h3>
<p>Not yet. The dashboard is only on your home network. Remote viewing is
planned.</p>
<h3 id="why-doesnt-use-my-current-location-work-on-my-phone">Why doesn't "Use my current location" work on my phone?</h3>
<p>Phones only share your location with secure (<code>https://</code>) pages. The board's
page is a plain <code>http://</code> page on your home network, so the phone blocks it
and the button tells you so — it isn't a setting you've missed. Type your
town in <strong>Place</strong> instead.</p>
<h3 id="why-is-a-plane-i-can-see-not-on-the-list">Why is a plane I can see not on the list?</h3>
<p>It may be outside your scan area or altitude filters, not broadcasting
ADS-B, or out of range of OpenSky's receivers (more common for low-flying
aircraft far from a receiver).</p>
<h3 id="what-does-closest-approach-mean">What does "closest approach" mean?</h3>
<p>If the aircraft keeps its current heading and speed, how long until it's
nearest to you and how close it'll get. <code>--</code> means it's moving away.</p>
<h3 id="can-i-use-a-different-esp32-board">Can I use a different ESP32 board?</h3>
<p>Any ESP32 with a 128×64 SSD1306 OLED on pins 21/22 should work. Other pin
layouts and screens are planned — see <a href="/flightboard/hardware/#other-boards">Hardware</a>.</p>
<h3 id="how-do-i-update-it">How do I update it?</h3>
<p>The board tells you when a new version is out (a small arrow on the screen
and a banner on the dashboard). Follow <a href="/flightboard/update/">Update your board</a> — it
takes about 3 minutes and keeps your settings. Updating over Wi-Fi with no

c:Tac8,<h1 id="first-time-setup">First-time setup</h1>
<h2 id="1-connect-it-to-your-wi-fi">1. Connect it to your Wi-Fi</h2>
<p>The first time it starts (or whenever it can't find a known network), the
board creates its own Wi-Fi hotspot.</p>
<ol>
<li>The screen shows <strong>WI-FI SETUP — JOIN THIS HOTSPOT: FLIGHTBOARD-SETUP</strong>.</li>
<li>On your phone, join the Wi-Fi network <strong>FlightBoard-setup</strong>.</li>
<li>A setup page opens automatically. (If not, open a browser and go to
<code>192.168.4.1</code>.)</li>
<li>Tap <strong>Configure WiFi</strong>, pick your home network and enter its password.</li>
<li>The board saves it, connects, and shows its <strong>address</strong> (e.g.
<code>192.168.1.42</code>) with a <strong>QR code</strong> for 10 seconds.</li>
</ol>
<p>The hotspot stays open for 3 minutes. If nobody sets it up, the board
restarts and tries again.</p>
<blockquote>
<p>The board works on <strong>2.4 GHz</strong> Wi-Fi only.</p>
</blockquote>
<h2 id="2-open-the-dashboard">2. Open the dashboard</h2>
<p>On a phone, just <strong>scan the QR code</strong> on the board's screen with the camera.
Or, on any phone or computer on the same Wi-Fi, open a browser and go to the
address shown, e.g. <code>http://192.168.1.42</code>.</p>
<p>Forgot the address? <strong>Hold the button for about 1 second</strong> and let go — the
address and QR code come back for 15 seconds.</p>
<h2 id="3-set-your-location">3. Set your location</h2>
<p>The board starts out watching the sky over Shannon, Ireland.</p>
<ol>
<li>Open <strong>Settings</strong> (the ⚙ next to "Scanning", or the menu).</li>
<li>Type your town or city in <strong>Place</strong> and press <strong>Find</strong>, or enter latitude
and longitude yourself.</li>
<li>Press <strong>Save changes</strong>. The board refreshes within a few seconds.</li>
</ol>
<p>If the place can't be found, nothing is changed and you'll see
"Place not found" — try a nearby larger town, or add the country
("Ennis, Ireland").</p>
<p>"Use my current location" works on laptops, but phones usually block it on
local web pages — see <a href="/flightboard/faq/#why-doesnt-use-my-current-location-work-on-my-phone">FAQ</a>.</p>
<h2 id="4-recommended-add-a-free-opensky-account">4. (Recommended) Add a free OpenSky account</h2>
<p>Without an account the board can make about 400 requests a day, which runs
out after a few hours at the default refresh rate. A free account gives
about 4,000 a day. See <a href="/flightboard/opensky-account/">OpenSky account</a>.</p>

c:Tc79,<h1 id="troubleshooting">Troubleshooting</h1>
<h2 id="flashing">Flashing</h2>
<p><strong>No port appears when I plug the board in.</strong>
Almost always the cable or a hub. Use a USB <strong>data</strong> cable (many are
charge-only) plugged <strong>directly</strong> into the computer, not through a hub or
dock. Try another cable before installing drivers.</p>
<p><strong>Upload fails with "No more data to read" or times out.</strong>
Set <strong>Tools → Upload Speed</strong> to <strong>115200</strong>. Faster speeds are unreliable on
these boards.</p>
<p><strong>"Failed to install platform esp32".</strong>
You probably have the ESP32 boards URL listed twice in Preferences. Keep one
copy, restart the IDE, remove the ESP32 platform, then install it once.</p>
<p><strong>"Sketch too big".</strong>
Set <strong>Tools → Partition Scheme</strong> to <strong>Huge APP</strong>.</p>
<p><strong>Compile error mentioning WiFiManager callbacks.</strong>
Check you installed <strong>WiFiManager by tzapu</strong> and not a similarly named
library.</p>
<h2 id="wi-fi">Wi-Fi</h2>
<p><strong>The setup hotspot doesn't appear.</strong>
Keep holding the button for 5 s (a bar fills; let go early to cancel) to
clear saved Wi-Fi. The screen will say
<strong>WI-FI SETUP — JOIN THIS HOTSPOT: FLIGHTBOARD-SETUP</strong>.</p>
<p><strong>It won't join my network.</strong>
The ESP32 only supports <strong>2.4 GHz</strong> Wi-Fi. If your router combines 2.4 and
5 GHz under one name, try enabling a separate 2.4 GHz network.</p>
<p><strong>I set a static IP and now I can't reach it.</strong>
Keep holding the button for 5 s to reset Wi-Fi, then set it up again and clear the
static IP fields.</p>
<h2 id="data">Data</h2>
<p><strong>No aircraft are showing.</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Check the location in Settings.</li>
<li>Try a bigger <strong>Scan size</strong>, or check your altitude filters.</li>
<li>At night, quiet areas can genuinely be empty.</li>
<li>Look for a rate-limit notice — you may be out of credits.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>"Rate limited" / it stops updating after a few hours.</strong>
You've used your daily OpenSky credits. Add a free
<a href="/flightboard/opensky-account/">OpenSky account</a> or poll less often.</p>
<p><strong>Route or aircraft type is blank.</strong>
Not every flight is in the public route and aircraft databases.</p>
<h2 id="dashboard">Dashboard</h2>
<p><strong>The page won't load.</strong>
Make sure your phone/laptop is on the same Wi-Fi as the board. Hold the
button ~1 s and let go to see the address and a QR code.</p>
<p><strong>No sound.</strong>
Tap anywhere on the page first — browsers block sound until you interact —
then check the 🔔 / ♪ buttons.</p>
<p><strong>"Use my current location" says permission denied.</strong>
Expected on phones — see the <a href="/flightboard/faq/#why-doesnt-use-my-current-location-work-on-my-phone">FAQ</a>.
Type your town instead.</p>

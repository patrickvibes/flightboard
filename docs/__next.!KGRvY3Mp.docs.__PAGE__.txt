c:T883,<h1 id="flight-board">Flight Board</h1>
<p><strong>See what's flying over you, right now.</strong> Flight Board is a small ESP32
board with a 0.96" OLED screen that shows live aircraft overhead — callsign,
distance, altitude, speed and when each one will pass closest. It also serves
a full dashboard to any browser on your home network: a live map, a cockpit
view, an "overhead now" alert and more.</p>
<p>No app to install, no subscription. Data comes from the
<a href="https://opensky-network.org">OpenSky Network</a>, a community-run network of
aircraft receivers.</p>
<h2 id="what-you-get">What you get</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>On the device:</strong> one flight at a time on the OLED, stepping with the
button or rotating automatically, with a beep and LED flash for new
arrivals, close approaches and emergency codes.</li>
<li><strong>In your browser:</strong> a live flight board, map, per-flight details (airline,
route, aircraft type), a full-screen cockpit instrument panel, traffic
history, session records and a wall-display kiosk mode.</li>
</ul>

A departures board for the sky over your house.

Flight Board is an ESP32 with a small screen that lists every aircraft crossing a patch of sky around you, live from the OpenSky Network. Leave it running all week and it starts keeping score.

Every week is a new round.

Rounds run Monday to Sunday on Dublin time. Your board scores four records as it watches, and the best in each league takes the week's trophy.

Same sky rules for every board.

At the start of each round your board asks the server for its scan radius and interval. The answer is signed, so changing the sketch changes nothing.

Your board still does the tracking and sends its own records. We check them against our own count of the same patch of sky, and each finished week is sealed and chained to the one before it.

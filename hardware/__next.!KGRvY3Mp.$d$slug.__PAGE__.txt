<h2 id="other-boards">Other boards</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Other ESP32 boards with an OLED</strong> (e.g. Heltec, TTGO) often use different

board profiles is planned.</li>

<li><strong>ESP32-S2 / S3 / C3</strong> need a separate build and aren't supported yet.</li>

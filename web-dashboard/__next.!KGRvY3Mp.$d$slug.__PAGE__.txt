c:Te32,<h1 id="web-dashboard">Web dashboard</h1>
<p>Open <code>http://&#x3C;board-address>/</code> on any device on the same Wi-Fi. The page
updates itself every 5 seconds — no need to refresh.</p>
<h2 id="the-board">The board</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Scanning</strong> shows your area and coordinates.</li>
<li><strong>Stats line</strong> with the nearest, fastest and highest aircraft.</li>
<li><strong>Traffic sparkline</strong> — how many aircraft were in your area over the last
60 updates, with the peak. Kept in your browser between visits.</li>
<li><strong>Flight list</strong> — callsign, distance, altitude (▲ climbing / ▼ descending),
speed and closest approach, nearest first. The <strong>◉</strong> marker is brightest
for the closest aircraft and blinks when a flight is new.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Tap a flight</strong> to open its details: airline, route (from → to) and
aircraft type/registration where known. From there you can:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Lock</strong> it to the OLED screen (tap again to unlock),</li>
<li><strong>✈ Enter cockpit</strong> for the instrument view.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="live-map">Live map</h2>
<p>Menu → <strong>Live map</strong>. Each aircraft is an arrow at its position, pointing the
way it's heading. Tap one to select it; the selected aircraft has a white
glow.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Colour shows altitude:</strong> orange near the ground, then yellow, green and
blue, up to violet at 40,000 ft (12,000 m) and above. Aircraft on the
ground are grey; an emergency is red. The key is in the bottom-left
corner.</li>
<li><strong>Trails</strong> show where each aircraft has flown since it entered your area:
faint where it came in, solid at its current position. Each part of the
trail is coloured by the altitude it was flying at then, so climbs and
descents show as a change of colour.</li>
<li>Trails are kept by the open dashboard page, from the moment it loads, for
the last 60 updates. Reloading the page starts them again.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="cockpit">Cockpit</h2>
<p>A full-screen instrument panel for one flight: airspeed dial, heading
compass and altimeter, updating live. <strong>Exit cockpit</strong> to return.</p>
<h2 id="overhead-now">Overhead now</h2>
<p>When an aircraft comes within <strong>3 km</strong> of you (horizontally), a lime
<strong>OVERHEAD NOW</strong> banner appears at the top of the page and follows that
aircraft. It's brightest when the plane is within about 1.2 km and fades as
it moves away. A rising chime plays as it enters (if sound is on).</p>
<p>The banner follows the board's own live estimate, so it appears at the same
moment as the board's OVERHEAD screen, even between OpenSky updates.</p>
<h2 id="sound">Sound</h2>
<p>Two buttons in the top bar:</p>
<ul>
<li>🔔 <strong>Arrival chime</strong> — a soft two-tone ding for each new aircraft.</li>
<li>♪ <strong>Ambient audio</strong> — a quiet background drone.</li>
</ul>
<p>Browsers only allow sound after you've tapped the page once.</p>
<h2 id="this-session">This session</h2>
<p>Peak traffic (and when), highest and fastest aircraft seen, countries and
unique callsigns since you opened the page.</p>
<h2 id="kiosk-mode-wall-display">Kiosk mode (wall display)</h2>
<p>Add <code>?kiosk</code> to the address, e.g. <code>http://192.168.1.42/?kiosk</code>. Menus and
buttons are hidden, text is larger, and a highlight moves down the list
every 4 seconds. Ideal for an old tablet on the wall.</p>

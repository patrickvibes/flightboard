# Update your board

Your board told you there's a new version. Updating takes about 3 minutes and
**keeps your Wi-Fi, location and settings**.

1. Unplug the board from its power supply and plug it into a computer with a
   USB data cable.
2. Open [Install from your browser](install.md) in **Chrome** or **Edge**.
3. Click **Connect**, choose the board's port, then **Install Flight Board**.
4. Leave **Erase device unticked**, and install.
5. When it's done, unplug it, put it back where it lives, and power it up.

The dashboard footer and the boot screen show the version you're running.

## Don't want update notices?
Turn off **Settings → Firmware → Check for firmware updates**. The board
never installs anything on its own — it only tells you a new version exists.

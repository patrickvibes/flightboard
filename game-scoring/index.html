How the game scores

Game mode is coming soon. This page explains how it will work. Your board keeps doing everything it does today; game mode adds a weekly competition on top, against friends in a league or everyone on the world table.

Every week is a new round

Rounds run Monday 00:00 to Sunday 23:59, Dublin time. Your board watches the sky all week and keeps four records. When the round ends, the week is sealed, trophies are handed out, and everyone starts again from zero on Monday.

You don't need to do anything during the week. Just leave the board running.

The four records

Record What counts What helps
Fastest The highest ground speed of any aircraft your board saw, boosted by how close it came (see the rings below) Jet-stream nights, and planes passing close by
Highest The greatest altitude of any aircraft your board saw, boosted the same way Business jets often fly higher than airliners
Most tracked How many different flights (callsigns) your board logged this week Keeping your board switched on: uptime matters more than location
Busiest sky The most aircraft inside your sky window at the same moment Evening transatlantic waves often set this one

Each record shows the flight that earned it, for example "1,084 km/h ×5 · UAE41 · Boston → Dubai · Wed 21:14".

The target rings

Your board's patch of sky is a target: four rings around your location, out to 60 km. Distances are measured along the ground, so a plane counts as "overhead" when it's directly above you at any height.

Ring Distance from your board Boost
Outer 30 to 60 km ×1
Inner 15 to 30 km ×2
Centre 3 to 15 km ×3
Overhead within 3 km ×5

Each aircraft gets the boost of the closest ring it reached, and only that one: a plane that flies right over you is ×5, not 1 + 2 + 3 + 5.

The boost applies to Fastest and Highest. So a jet doing 1,000 km/h straight over your house (1,000 × 5) beats one doing 1,100 km/h at the edge of your window (1,100 × 1). Tables always show the real speed or altitude with its boost next to it, never a made-up number.

Most tracked and Busiest sky have no boost: they reward a board that's always on and a busy patch of sky.

When a plane crosses the overhead ring, your board flashes +5 OVERHEAD and plays its chime, as it does now.

Planes between checks

Your board checks for aircraft about once a minute, and a jet can travel 13 km in that time. So the game looks at the path a plane flew between two checks, not just the two dots: if the line between them passes over you, it counts as overhead. If a plane disappears for more than a few minutes, the game doesn't guess where it went.

Planes that hang around

A plane circling or holding nearby counts once. It can only count again after it has been outside your window for 30 minutes.

League tables

In a league, everyone is ranked in each of the four records. Your league position adds up your four places: for example 1st + 3rd + 2nd + 4th = 10. The lowest total wins. If two players tie, the one who tracked more flights goes ahead.

At the end of the week, the leader in each record wins that week's trophy for the league. Trophies stay in your trophy cabinet for good.

The world table

Every paired board is also on the world table, ranked in each record. On the world table, player callsigns are hidden (you appear as something like ANON · 7Q3K) and only your country is shown, never your location.

Fair play

  • Same rules for every board. At the start of each round your board fetches the round's rules (window size, ring sizes, how often to check) from the game server. The rules are signed, so editing the board's software changes nothing.
  • Checked against our own data. Your board still does the tracking and sends its records. The server checks them against its own copy of the same OpenSky data for your patch of sky. Results that match show as Matched. Anything that doesn't is In review: it stays on the table, but can't win a trophy until it's cleared.
  • Sealed weeks. Each finished week is sealed with a fingerprint that is chained to the week before, so past results can't be quietly changed.

Questions

Does living under a flight path win everything? It helps with Busiest sky, but not much else. Fastest and Highest reward planes that come close, and Most tracked rewards keeping the board on. A board in a quiet spot can still take trophies.

What if my board is offline for a while? It simply doesn't see planes while it's off. Most tracked is the record that suffers most. Nothing is lost from the rest of the week.

Do I need an OpenSky account? Yes, for game mode. It lets your board check often enough for a fair game. See OpenSky account & usage limits.

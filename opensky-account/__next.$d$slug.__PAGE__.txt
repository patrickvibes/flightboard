b:Tc81,<h1 id="opensky-account--usage-limits">OpenSky account &#x26; usage limits</h1>
<p>Flight Board gets its data from the <a href="https://opensky-network.org">OpenSky Network</a>.
OpenSky limits how many requests each user can make per day, counted in
<strong>credits</strong>. Each update of your area costs one credit.</p>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Access</th>
<th>Credits per day</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td>Anonymous (no account)</td>
<td>~400</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Free OpenSky account</td>
<td>~4,000</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<h2 id="how-long-will-my-credits-last-pacing">How long will my credits last? (pacing)</h2>
<p>Credits reset every day at <strong>00:00 UTC</strong> (midnight in Ireland in winter, 1 am in summer). With
<strong>pacing</strong> on (the default), the board does the maths for you: after each
update it divides the credits it has left by the time until the reset, and
slows down just enough that they last — so it <strong>never runs dry mid-day</strong>.
It never goes faster than your <strong>Poll every</strong> setting.</p>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Access</th>
<th>Credits per day</th>
<th>What pacing does at the default 60 s</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td>Anonymous</td>
<td>~400</td>
<td>Settles at about <strong>every 3–4 minutes</strong>, all day</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Free account</td>
<td>~4,000</td>
<td>Usually no change — 60 s fits comfortably</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<p>When the board is pacing, the dashboard says <strong>every 216s (paced)</strong> and the
board's usage screen (double-tap) says <strong>PACED 216S</strong> instead of
<strong>EVERY 60S</strong>. Bigger scan areas (over 5° across) cost more credits per
update, and pacing allows for that too.</p>
<p>You can turn pacing off in <strong>Settings → Display</strong>. Without it, at 60 s an
anonymous board uses its ~400 credits in about 7 hours, then waits for the
reset.</p>
<p><strong>In short: get a free account</strong> if you want frequent updates all day.</p>
<h2 id="adding-your-account">Adding your account</h2>
<ol>
<li>Create a free account at <a href="https://opensky-network.org">opensky-network.org</a>.</li>
<li>In your account page, create an <strong>API client</strong>. OpenSky gives you a
<code>credentials.json</code> file containing a <code>clientId</code> and <code>clientSecret</code>.</li>
<li>On the Flight Board dashboard, open <strong>Settings → OpenSky account</strong>.</li>
<li>Choose the <code>credentials.json</code> file and press <strong>Load</strong>.</li>
<li>The status changes to <strong>Authenticated as …</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<p>The credentials are stored on the board only. The secret is never shown
again on the page. To go back to anonymous, press <strong>Remove credentials</strong>.</p>
<blockquote>
<p><strong>Keep <code>credentials.json</code> private.</strong> Don't post it or share it. If it's
ever exposed, delete that API client on OpenSky and create a new one.</p>
</blockquote>

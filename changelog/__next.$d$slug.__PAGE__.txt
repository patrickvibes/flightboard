<p>What's new in each Flight Board firmware release. Newest first.</p>
<h2 id="unreleased">[Unreleased]</h2>
<ul>
<li>Live map: aircraft arrows now point the way the plane is actually
heading (they were a quarter-turn off).</li>
<li>Live map: aircraft and their trails are coloured by altitude, and trails
fade from where the plane entered your area to solid at its position.</li>
<li>"Use my current location" now explains why phones won't share location
with the board's page, instead of a bare "Permission denied".</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="120---2026-11-07">[1.2.0] - 2026-11-07</h2>
<p>Your OpenSky credits now last all day.</p>
<ul>
<li>Credits now last all day: the board paces its updates so OpenSky's daily
allowance runs until the midnight (UTC) reset instead of running out in
the afternoon. Without an account it updates every few minutes, all day.
Can be turned off in Settings.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="110---2026-11-07">[1.1.0] - 2026-11-07</h2>
<p>New look for the board's screen.</p>
<ul>
<li>New screens on the board: a cleaner flight view with airline and route
codes, a radar "quiet sky" when nothing's around, close-approach and
emergency alert screens, and a "no data" screen that says why and when
it'll retry.</li>
<li>Hold the button for 1 s to get a QR code that opens the dashboard.</li>
<li>Wi-Fi reset now shows a countdown bar: let go to cancel; it fires at 5 s.</li>
<li>Overhead screen redesigned with a closeness meter.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="100---2026-11-07">[1.0.0] - 2026-11-07</h2>
<p>First versioned release.</p>
<ul>
<li>Overhead moment on the board: a chime, a screen flash and an OVERHEAD NOW
screen when a plane passes within 3 km, with a live countdown.</li>
<li>Soft tick while a plane is overhead (can be turned off in Settings).</li>
<li>The board now tells you when a new version is out (Settings → Firmware).</li>
<li>Install and update from your browser, no Arduino IDE needed.</li>
<li>Cleaner traffic chart: the label no longer overlaps the line.</li>

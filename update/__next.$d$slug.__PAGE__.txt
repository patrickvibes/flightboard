b:T458,<h1 id="update-your-board">Update your board</h1>
<p>Your board told you there's a new version. Updating takes about 3 minutes and
<strong>keeps your Wi-Fi, location and settings</strong>.</p>
<ol>
<li>Unplug the board from its power supply and plug it into a computer with a
USB data cable.</li>
<li>Open <a href="/flightboard/install/">Install from your browser</a> in <strong>Chrome</strong> or <strong>Edge</strong>.</li>
<li>Click <strong>Connect</strong>, choose the board's port, then <strong>Install Flight Board</strong>.</li>
<li>Leave <strong>Erase device unticked</strong>, and install.</li>
<li>When it's done, unplug it, put it back where it lives, and power it up.</li>
</ol>
<p>The dashboard footer and the boot screen show the version you're running.</p>
